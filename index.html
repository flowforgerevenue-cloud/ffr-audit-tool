📖 How To Read The Four Lines

Status Quo — red dashed line. This is the trajectory if the identified revenue friction remains unresolved. It assumes 3% annual growth, so it represents the business continuing on its current path without a targeted recovery effort.

Conservative — orange line. This shows the outcome of a cautious recovery, using an 18% annual growth assumption after addressing the identified friction.

Base Case — lime line. This represents the expected recovery scenario, using a 35% annual growth assumption when the recommended fix is implemented effectively.

Full Upside — green line. This represents the highest modeled recovery path at 70% annual growth. In plain commercial terms, the widening gap between these lines shows the value of changing the revenue trajectory earlier rather than allowing the opportunity cost to compound.
